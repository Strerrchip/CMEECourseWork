In these runs, the large mixed population had the smaller mean absolute ratio deviation from one over months 1-120. Larger populations generally have lower relative demographic noise, making empirical mean trajectories more stable. For this linear model, the deterministic trajectory represents the expected stochastic population for either initial condition. Finite-simulation averages can differ from that expectation. Extinct populations must remain in the average as zeros; averaging only survivors would describe a different, conditional quantity.
