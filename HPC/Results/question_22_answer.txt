The initial condition affects the early dynamics, but its influence is expected to weaken after sufficient burn-in. Both simulations have the same community size and speciation probability and approach the same stationary distribution. Their mean octave distributions should therefore be similar, although finite stochastic samples will differ. Readings from one continuing simulation can remain correlated; sampling every 20 generations does not guarantee independence. These two trajectories illustrate reduced initial-condition effects, rather than proving that 200 generations are sufficient in every run.
